Iterative Editing for Creative Control

Transform your video editing workflow with powerful iterative tools that put you in control of every detail.

Today's Workflow

Current video creation starts with uploading media and describing your vision with a single prompt. While powerful, it's a one-shot process that can feel like a black box.

2

Edit Your Outline

Take control with an editable outline view. Review the AI-generated structure and make changes before rendering. See exactly what's planned for your video.

3

Enhanced Outline Editing

Drag and drop sections to reorder your story. Click into any section to edit its content, timing, and media. The power of modular editing meets AI assistance.

4

Special Commands

Add polish with dedicated tools for b-roll, titles, and transitions. Professional touches are just a click away, perfectly integrated into your workflow.

5

Natural Language Prompting

Use everyday language to refine your edit. "Make it more upbeat", "Add a dramatic pause", "Emphasize the product shot" - the AI understands and iterates.
